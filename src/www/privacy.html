Company Check

  • What my server keeps: when you book, the booking service sends my server a copy of the booking. My server keeps only the company name and website as you typed them, your email's domain (the part after the @), scrambled (hashed) codes made from your email address and from the booking's ID, the booking times, the number of people booked, and the check's own reference number, codes and results. It does not keep your name, your full email address, or your other answers.
  • Use: to spot fake bookings, and to learn about the company before the call. The check compares your email's domain with the company's website. It looks up how long the company's domain has been registered, its public business listing, and its public filings if it is publicly traded. It also gets a short AI-written summary of public facts about the company. The check is about the company. It does not search for you by name.
  • Recipients: domain registries receive web domain names. The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission's public filings database may be asked for the company's filings, only if its name matches a publicly traded company. Google may receive the company name, to find its business listing. Anthropic, an AI service, receives the company name and website, and searches the web for public facts about the company. Anthropic's business terms bar it from training its AI on what it receives.
  • Result: a score and a short summary, emailed only to me through Amazon SES. The email does not include your name, your full email address, or your other answers.
  • No automatic decisions: the check never cancels, blocks or changes a booking, and its score is not shown on the booking page. A low score only means I take a closer look myself, for example on LinkedIn. Any decision about a booking is mine.
  • Retention: working data is deleted once the summary is sent, and is never kept more than 7 days. The check record (the hashed email code, the company's web domain, the score and its label, and the times) and the hashed booking-ID code are deleted after 30 days. The summary email is kept with my other email, as a business record.
  • Link, not embed: the booking page stays on the booking service's own site, and this site only links to it. The check runs between the booking service and my server. It adds no scripts or cookies to this site, and nothing to your browser.
  • Earlier bookings: bookings made before October 6, 2026 are not checked.
